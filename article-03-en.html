From Dialogue to Concrete Action: CCIPU Ukraine Desk

President of the Italian Chamber of Commerce for Ukraine (Camera di Commercio Italiana per l’Ucraina, CCIPU), Mr Renato Walter Tonni, signed the Letter of Authorization.

On 23 June 2026 in Turin, the President of the Italian Chamber of Commerce for Ukraine (Camera di Commercio Italiana per l’Ucraina, CCIPU), Mr Renato Walter Tonni, signed a Letter of Authorization officially confirming the establishment of Camera di Commercio Italiana per l’Ucraina — Ukraine Desk operated by CONSULT HUB. The legal basis for Ukraine Desk’s activities is the Operational Delegation Mandate signed by the Italian Chamber of Commerce for Ukraine and Ivan Synetar, Director of Expert Consulting Centre “Consult Hub” LLC.

Under these documents:

- Ivan Synetar is the National Delegate for Ukraine, Head of Ukraine Desk and CCIPU’s official representative for developing institutional and operational activities in Ukraine.

- Expert Consulting Centre “Consult Hub” LLC is CCIPU’s official local operating partner in Ukraine.

The primary mission of CCIPU Ukraine Desk is to provide a practical mechanism for developing economic, trade, investment and institutional cooperation between Ukraine and Italy.

Our work focuses on establishing direct business contacts, developing partnerships and implementing joint projects that meet the needs of businesses in both countries.

What can businesses gain from working with CCIPU Ukraine Desk?

For Ukrainian businesses: new opportunities to enter the Italian market, access technology and develop international partnerships.

- Entering the Italian market. Identifying potential buyers, distributors and commercial partners to support exports.

- Access to Italian manufacturers and technologies. Selecting suppliers of equipment, materials and technological solutions based on companies’ production and commercial needs.

- Developing industrial cooperation. Building partnerships for joint manufacturing, technology localisation and industrial projects.

- Direct business negotiations. Organising targeted meetings with Italian companies, industry associations and relevant institutions.



For Italian businesses: structured support for entering the Ukrainian market, identifying reliable partners and participating in promising projects.

- Finding customers and partners in Ukraine. Establishing contacts with potential clients, distributors and Ukrainian companies.

- Assessing market opportunities. Identifying promising segments and areas of cooperation in line with current needs in the Ukrainian market.

- Participating in recovery and development projects. Connecting with Ukrainian communities, companies and initiators of infrastructure and industrial projects.

- Organising business engagement. Preparing B2B and B2G meetings, conducting negotiations and supporting the development of joint project initiatives.

Why should businesses contact CCIPU Ukraine Desk?

Entering international markets and finding partners require time, trusted contacts and an understanding of how business is conducted in another country.

CCIPU Ukraine Desk provides a structured approach to international cooperation: from assessing a specific business request and identifying relevant partners to arranging direct negotiations and agreeing on next steps.

Within its mandate, Ukraine Desk engages with public authorities, local communities, chambers of commerce, business associations, universities and international organisations.
