CCIPU Ukraine Desk · 25.09.2026

CCIPU Roundtable at Its Turin Headquarters

CCIPU Ukraine Desk meets Italian businesses to discuss five practical areas for cooperation in Ukraine’s reconstruction.

On 25 September, during a working visit to Italy, Ivan Synetar, Head of CCIPU Ukraine Desk, held a roundtable at the Turin headquarters of the Camera di Commercio Italiana per l’Ucraina (CCIPU). Together with CCIPU President Walter Tonni and representatives of Italian businesses, participants discussed concrete opportunities for Italian companies to contribute to Ukraine’s reconstruction. The discussion was lively and focused, demonstrating a high level of interest. Five practical areas took centre stage.

1. Decentralised warehousing and logistics infrastructure

Russian attacks have damaged or destroyed many of Ukraine’s large logistics facilities. Rebuilding complexes of 50,000–100,000 m² takes time, is technically challenging and, given the security risks, is not always advisable.

Italian companies offer an alternative: rapidly deployable modular warehouses of 1,500, 3,000 and 5,000 m². Their modular, distributed and scalable design enables capacity to be restored quickly. SIMEST and SACE have already indicated their willingness in principle to explore financing mechanisms for such projects. We therefore see this as one of the areas with the greatest potential for rapid implementation.

2. Water treatment and restoration of water infrastructure

The first area involves mobile and containerised water treatment systems for frontline regions, where water supply infrastructure is repeatedly damaged.

The second involves Italian companies participating in the modernisation of water supply and wastewater systems in communities farther from the front line. Cooperation may range from equipment supply and works commissioned by municipalities to comprehensive reconstruction of water utilities through public-private partnerships.

3. Waste management

Participants discussed Italy’s experience in developing facilities for sorting, recycling and recovering energy from municipal waste. Such projects can address environmental challenges while strengthening regional energy resilience.

This requires an appropriate regulatory framework in Ukraine. We have already discussed the issue with the Ukrainian Parliament’s group for interparliamentary relations with the Italian Republic and received expressions of willingness to cooperate from Italian parliamentarians.

A separate opportunity is the recycling of construction waste. Italian companies have substantial expertise in this field, as a significant share of construction activity in Italy involves reconstruction and renovation.

4. Processing agricultural raw materials in Ukraine

Ukraine is an important supplier of grain to Italy’s animal feed industry. However, high maritime transport costs make shipping large volumes of unprocessed raw materials increasingly inefficient.

One solution is to move part of the processing directly to Ukraine, involving Italian equipment manufacturers in joint ventures. This would create new production facilities and jobs, increase the value added to Ukrainian products and reduce logistics costs for Italian partners.

5. Building materials and innovative structures

Participants considered the supply of Italian finishing materials for Ukrainian construction projects, as well as modern structural components that could replace steel structures in certain applications.

Companies specialising in water treatment, waste management, industrial equipment, construction technologies, fire protection systems, energy and the food industry expressed interest in further cooperation.

The main outcome was a series of working online meetings with Italian companies. Their purpose is to identify specific projects, Ukrainian partners, financing mechanisms and next steps.

We thank CCIPU President Walter Tonni, all roundtable participants and representatives of Italian businesses for a substantive dialogue and their readiness to work together.

Our mission at CCIPU Ukraine Desk is to turn Italian business interest into real projects, contracts and long-term partnerships with Ukraine. We continue building a bridge for economic cooperation — from ideas to tangible results.
