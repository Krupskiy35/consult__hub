AREAS OF EXPERTISE

Ourservices

01

Entering the Ukrainian market

We help international companies assess opportunities in the Ukrainian market, identify potential partners and develop a market-entry approach.

What the service includes:

  • Assessment of the target segment of the Ukrainian market.
  • Mapping potential partners.
  • Defining a market-entry model.
  • Preparing a negotiation plan.
Deliverables:segment assessment, potential partner map, market-entry model and negotiation plan.
02

International expansion for Ukrainian businesses

We help Ukrainian companies identify overseas partners, establish international business contacts and prepare cooperation opportunities to support growth.

What the service includes:

  • Preparing a business proposition profile for international partners.
  • Identifying overseas partners based on business needs.
  • Preparing business introductions and negotiations.
  • Supporting engagement with potential partners.
Deliverables:business proposition profile, shortlist of overseas partners, and preparation and support of business contacts.
03

Project preparation

We help businesses, communities and project initiators prepare projects for the next stages of implementation, identify stakeholders and develop a preliminary financial model.

What the service includes:

  • Developing the project concept.
  • Identifying potential participants and their roles.
  • Preparing preliminary project financials.
  • Compiling the required input information.
  • Preparing a plan for the next stage.
Deliverables:project concept, participants, preliminary financials, required input information and next-stage plan.
04

International partnership support

We coordinate cooperation between Ukrainian and international partners, help organize negotiations, agree on responsibilities and prepare the next steps.

What the service includes:

  • Organizing and coordinating negotiations.
  • Agreeing on the parties’ roles and responsibilities.
  • Engaging legal and technical specialists.
  • Coordinating ongoing interaction between partners.
Deliverables:coordinated negotiations, agreed responsibilities and engagement of legal and technical specialists.
05

Project implementation coordination

We provide practical support during project implementation, coordinate participants and monitor the completion of agreed tasks.

What the service includes:

  • Preparing a work plan.
  • Agreeing on tasks, owners and deadlines.
  • Coordinating interaction among project participants.
  • Monitoring completion of agreed tasks.
  • Preparing progress reports.
Deliverables:work plan, monitoring of agreed tasks, reporting and coordination between parties.
