FAQ / CONSULT HUB

Frequently asked questions about international business consulting

On international business development, partnerships and project support in Ukraine.

Discuss your inquiry ↗︎︎︎
01What requests can Consult Hub help with?

We help international companies enter the Ukrainian market and Ukrainian businesses build international partnerships. We support project preparation, negotiations and coordination between parties.

02How do you help international companies operate in Ukraine?

We assess opportunities in the Ukrainian market, identify potential partners, develop a market-entry model and negotiation plan, and coordinate agreed local work stages.

03Do you help Ukrainian businesses find international partners?

Yes. We help prepare offers for international markets, identify potential partners and organize further communication.

04Can I approach you with an early-stage project?

Yes. We help refine the concept, identify participants, prepare initial data and develop a preliminary economic assessment.

05How does cooperation with Consult Hub work?

First, we clarify the request and expected outcome. Then we assess feasibility, prepare negotiations and documents, agree on a work plan and coordinate implementation.

06What does the client receive?

Depending on the task: a market opportunity assessment, partner map, prepared project, organized negotiations or an agreed implementation plan.

07How do I start working with you?

Send a brief description of your request, objective and current project stage. We will define the scope and potential format of further cooperation.
