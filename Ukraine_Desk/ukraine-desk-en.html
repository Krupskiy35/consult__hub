ABOUT UKRAINE DESK

About Ukraine Desk

CCIPU Ukraine Desk is an institutional and operational platform of the Camera di Commercio Italiana per l'Ucraina, dedicated to advancing economic cooperation between Italy and Ukraine.

Ukraine Desk brings businesses and institutions together to strengthen bilateral economic ties. We clarify participants' needs, help identify suitable partners, facilitate business engagement and support the development of joint initiatives.

01 / MISSION

Mission

To establish ongoing, practical cooperation between Italian and Ukrainian stakeholders, turning an initial enquiry into a well-prepared meeting, clearly defined roles and an agreed action plan.

02 / FUNCTIONS

Core functions

Ukraine Desk receives and clarifies requests, identifies relevant stakeholders, facilitates B2B and B2G engagement, and supports business missions and sector events. Follow-up includes documenting agreements, assigning responsibilities and setting deadlines for next steps.

MANDATE

Mandate, relationship with CCIPU and the role of Consult Hub

Within its Italy–Ukraine activities, Consult Hub combines coordination with CCIPU, knowledge of the Ukrainian market and hands-on support for partnership initiatives.

Mandate

The Camera di Commercio Italiana per l'Ucraina — Ukraine Desk operated by CONSULT HUB was established under an operational delegation agreement between the Italian Chamber of Commerce for Ukraine and Ivan Synetar. This was confirmed by a letter of authorisation dated 23 June 2026, signed by the Chamber's President, Renato Walter Tonni.

Letter of authorisation

The Italian Chamber of Commerce for Ukraine's letter of authorisation dated 23 June 2026 designates CONSULT HUB as its official local operating partner in Ukraine and Ivan Synetar as the National Delegate for Ukraine and an official CCIPU representative.

SECTORS

Priority areas of cooperation

Water and infrastructure

Water supply, wastewater treatment and municipal solutions.

Energy and resilience

Distributed generation, energy efficiency and critical infrastructure solutions.

Industry and construction

Equipment, engineering, materials and localisation of production.

Logistics and processing

Warehousing infrastructure, cold-chain solutions, processing and packaging.

Bilateral trade

Market entry, distribution and development of import and export partnerships.

Investment readiness is a cross-cutting element of these areas. New topics are considered where there is a specific request, an engaged counterpart and a clear economic rationale.

PROCESS

From initial enquiry to an actionable project

  1. 01

    Clarifying the request: objectives, needs, geography, timeline and responsible party.

  2. 02

    Initial assessment: fit with our expertise, stakeholder readiness and key constraints.

  3. 03

    Choosing the approach: institutional coordination or a separate commercial service.

  4. 04

  5. 05

    Delivery and next steps: acceptance of deliverables and agreement on next steps.

IMPORTANT

Scope and limitations

Ukraine Desk helps organise and prepare cooperation. Contract execution and decisions by investors, banks, donors or public authorities remain the responsibility of the respective parties. Ukraine Desk does not sign contracts, assume financial obligations, provide binding guarantees or make political statements on behalf of CCIPU without prior written approval.
